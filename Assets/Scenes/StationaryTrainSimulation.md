# Stationary train simulation

## Add it to your own train

1. Select your train root: **GameObject > Train > Add Stationary Simulation to Selected Train**.
2. Adjust the created BoxCollider to cover the playable interior. Train Frame's local +Z is forward.
3. Keep the train stationary. Disable physical path followers and animations that move its colliders.
4. Give fixed floors, walls and furniture colliders. Loose props need dynamic Rigidbodies, gravity and suitable colliders.
5. Select the player with the existing Player script and CharacterController: **GameObject > Train > Add Stationary Passenger to Selected Player**.
6. Choose a journey mode and press Play. Save the train and influence box as a prefab to reuse the setup.

The influence box selects bodies by their center of mass. Affected Body Layers
filters them. Kinematic/held objects are unaffected. Overlapping boxes choose one
simulation so forces are not doubled. The carriage's existing Rigidbody becomes
kinematic; other scripts must not move it.

## Journey modes

| Mode | Customization |
| --- | --- |
| Random Journey | Seed, speed range, driving duration, station dwell, bend duration, maximum turn rate and uphill angle. Braking finishes before the dwell timer starts. |
| Timetable | Ordered named stages: duration, speed, turn rate, grade and roughness. Zero speed requests a stop; repeating is optional. |
| Manual | Cruise speed, manual turn and uphill angle, controlled by scripts or triggers. |
| Virtual Route | Assign Train Route waypoints to supply bends and hills without moving the carriage. Open routes stop at their endpoint. |

Times are simulation seconds. Timetable durations include acceleration/braking;
allow sufficient time to reach zero speed. Stop and emergency brake pause journey
progression. Reset Journey resets the simulation, not the props or player.

## Motion and player customization

Tune acceleration, normal/emergency braking, turn/grade response, bounce amplitude
and frequency, vibration amplitude and frequency, roughness and banking. Vibration
amplitudes are deliberately small because acceleration increases with frequency
squared. Object Force Multiplier controls overall strength, Maximum Acceleration
caps extreme forces, and Include Rotational Forces adds rotational corrections.

Ordinary gravity is provided by Unity. The simulation adds acceleration, braking,
bend forces, tilt gravity correction and heave/vibration. All velocities are relative
to the carriage; do not add the virtual train's world speed to objects.

Stationary Train Passenger exposes footing resistance, crouch grip, force strength,
slip damping, maximum slip speed, camera lean and camera bounce. Grounded footing
resists weak forces; strong forces cause sliding. Airborne players lack footing
resistance. The existing player's gravity and controls remain in use.

The floor stays fixed: hills and banking are simulated forces and camera feedback.
This assumes an upright player and is a gameplay approximation, not a full
arbitrary-gravity carriage simulation.

## Command boxes and gameplay

Use **Create Command Trigger Box** in the simulation Inspector, or add Train
Simulation Trigger to a separate object. Assign the simulation and choose Start,
Stop, EmergencyStop, SetSpeed, SetTurn or SetGrade. Move/resize its trigger box.
Default filtering allows CharacterControllers only; actor layers and Once Only
are configurable. Speed/turn/grade commands switch to Manual by default.

These boxes respond to the player's physical entry. They are not virtual stations;
use timetable stages for timed train stops. Activate() can be called through
UnityEvents; ResetTrigger() resets a once-only trigger.

Simulation methods: StartTrain(), StopTrain(), EmergencyStop(), SetCruiseSpeed(float),
SetManualTurn(float), SetManualGrade(float). On Departed and On Stopped events can
drive sound, doors, lighting or gameplay.

## Test scene

Open **StationaryTrainTest.unity** and press Play. The lab builds at runtime, with
a stationary carriage, bottles, crate, ball, and boarding platform. Its default
repeating timetable demonstrates departure, right/left bends, a rough uphill
section and a station stop. The old circular test environment is removed.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / mouse / Space | Move / look / jump |
| B | Toggle emergency brake |
| T | Stop/start |
| R | Reset journey, player and props |
| F1 | Overview camera |
| P | Switch timetable/random journey and reset |

Enter the red platform pad to brake and the green pad to depart. In Play mode,
select Stationary train influence box to customize the simulation.

Exterior scenery is separate: Speed, TravelDistance, TurnRate, Grade and
VerticalOffset are exposed for scenery/audio systems. Scenery scrolling is not
generated by this system.
